You can add your wallpaper via nitrogen at ~/.config/nitrogen
Also you can customize starship at ~/.config/starship.toml
To remove starship: 
sudo rm /usr/local/bin/starship
delete eval "$(starship init bash) form .bashcr
rm -rf ~/.config/starship.toml
To delete fronts:
rm -rf ~/.local/share/fonts/JetBrainsMono*
fc-cache -f -v

/usr/bin/openbox-session
